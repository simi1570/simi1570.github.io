WikiGolf Privacy Policy

Last Updated: February 8, 2026

WikiGolf ("the App") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and how we manage it.

1. Information We Collect

The App may collect the following information:

  • Gameplay Statistics: In-game data such as play count, scores, and achievements
  • Device Information: Device type, OS version, advertising identifier (for ad delivery)
  • App Usage: Crash logs, error reports (for app quality improvement)

2. How We Use Information

The collected information is used for the following purposes:

  • Providing game functionality and improving user experience
  • Displaying personalized advertisements (via Google AdMob)
  • Improving the app, fixing bugs, and developing new features
  • Analyzing statistical information and improving service quality

3. Information Sharing with Third Parties

The App uses the following third-party services and may share information with them:

  • Google AdMob: Shares device identifiers and advertising-related information for ad delivery
    Google Privacy Policy
  • Google Play / App Store: Shares billing information for in-app purchase processing
    Google Privacy Policy

4. Data Storage and Management

  • Game Statistics Data: Stored locally on the device and deleted when the app is uninstalled
  • Advertising Identifier: Managed according to Google AdMob policies
  • Crash Logs: Deleted after a certain period following problem resolution

5. User Rights and Choices

Users have the following rights:

  • Data Deletion: All locally stored data can be deleted by uninstalling the app
  • Ad Personalization: Personalized ads can be disabled by resetting the advertising identifier in device settings
    • Android: Settings → Google → Ads → Reset advertising ID
    • iOS: Settings → Privacy → Advertising → Reset personalized ads
  • Ad Removal: Ads can be completely removed through in-app purchase (¥490)

6. Children's Privacy

The App is not intended for children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided personal information, please contact us at the address below.

7. Security

The App takes commercially reasonable measures to protect collected information from unauthorized access, loss, misuse, and alteration. However, please understand that no method of transmission over the Internet or data storage method is 100% secure.

8. Changes to Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as necessary. If there are significant changes, we will post the new Privacy Policy on this page and update the last updated date. We recommend that you check this page regularly.

9. Consent

By using the App, you are deemed to have agreed to this Privacy Policy.
